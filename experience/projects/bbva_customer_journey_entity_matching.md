# BBVA — Customer Journey Reconstruction & Entity Matching

## Executive summary

Advanced investigation analytics combining transactional, contractual and location-related data to reconstruct customer journeys and connect entities / events that were difficult to analyze in isolated systems.

## Business problem

Investigations can fail when relevant evidence is fragmented across transactions, contracts, locations, counterparties or inconsistent identifiers. Analysts need a coherent view of how events and entities connect over time.

## Objective

Create analytical logic that reconstructs customer / event journeys and matches related entities so operational or compliance teams can surface stronger investigation candidates.

## José Carlos's ownership

- integrated heterogeneous banking data sources
- reconstructed event sequences / customer journeys
- developed matching logic for entities with inconsistent or partially structured identifiers
- used text / NLP-oriented normalization where applicable
- combined transactional, contractual and geospatial context
- designed prioritization outputs for investigation teams
- translated analytical findings into interpretable cases for operations / compliance
