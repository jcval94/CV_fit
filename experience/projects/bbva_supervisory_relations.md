# Supervisory Relations To-Be Architecture

## Summary

A 2026 process-architecture initiative for regulatory operations, combining workflow/state design, SLAs, role clarity, Google Workspace automation and proposed LLM assistance.

## Business problem

The existing regulatory workflow relied on manual capture, ambiguous status definitions and limited visibility. The goal was to define a more traceable operating model that could support faster handling, explicit escalation and later automation.

## Contribution and ownership

Evidence supports technical leadership in:

- mapping the As-Is process and designing the To-Be workflow
- defining ticket states and transitions
- parameterizing SLAs and escalation logic
- documenting roles and responsibilities through RACI
- producing flow diagrams and executive infographics
- proposing Gemini/LLM assistance for summarizing regulatory changes and pre-assessing possible impact
- connecting Google Sheets, Forms, Apps Script, workflow tooling and reporting concepts

## Lifecycle

The design was approved for phased implementation. Time reductions were projected targets, not fully validated production outcomes at the source cutoff.

## Technologies and artifacts

- Google Sheets and Forms
- Apps Script
- Google Workspace workflow tooling
- Looker/reporting concepts
- Gemini / LLM-assisted semantic analysis
- RACI, state models and process diagrams

## Approved public narrative

> Led the To-Be design of a regulatory workflow with explicit states, SLAs, escalation and Google Workspace automation, incorporating proposed Gemini assistance for semantic change analysis.

## Boundaries

- Do not report projected SLA improvements as achieved production results.
- Do not claim that every proposed AI component was deployed.
- Preserve the distinction between process architecture, approved implementation plan and live operation.
