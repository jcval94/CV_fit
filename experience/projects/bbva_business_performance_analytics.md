# BBVA — Business Performance & Operating Model Analytics

## Executive summary

Strategic analytics work for Remote Banking and related operating-model decisions, focused on consistent KPI definitions, productivity measurement, trend analysis, benchmark comparisons and executive decision support.

## Business problem

Leadership needed a reliable view of how the operating model was performing over time and how results compared with relevant reference groups. Metric definitions, exclusions and comparison methodology materially affected interpretation.

## Objective

Create a repeatable KPI and benchmark framework that could explain business evolution and support decisions about productivity, performance and operating-model effectiveness.

## José Carlos's ownership

- defined and consolidated business KPIs
- integrated operational, customer and financial-performance inputs
- analyzed trends across monthly / quarterly periods
- designed benchmark and comparison-group logic
- documented metric definitions and exclusions
- prepared executive reporting and analytical narratives
- translated quantitative findings into recommendations for senior stakeholders

## Analytical themes

- KPI design
- business performance analytics
- productivity measurement
- benchmark / comparison-group analysis
- trend analysis
- executive reporting
- operating-model analytics

## Impact

The work documented material performance improvements and provided a consistent analytical basis for leadership decisions.
