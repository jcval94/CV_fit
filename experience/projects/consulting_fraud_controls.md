# Management Solutions — Fraud / Risk Control Redesign

## Executive summary

Consulting engagement focused on redesigning risk / fraud controls and prioritization logic so analysts could concentrate effort on higher-value cases and improve operational review capacity.

## Business problem

Existing control workflows required substantial manual review and did not prioritize cases efficiently enough. The analytical challenge was to improve control effectiveness without sacrificing interpretability or operational usability.

## Objective

Redesign monitoring and prioritization logic using quantitative evidence, risk signals and operational constraints to increase the efficiency of case review.
