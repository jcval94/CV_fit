# Management Solutions — Large-Scale Data Migration, Reconciliation & QA

## Executive summary

Large-scale data-transformation engagement focused on migration, reconciliation and quality assurance across platforms, with emphasis on consistency, traceability and control integrity.

## Business problem

Large data migrations can create silent inconsistencies across systems, fields and business definitions. A successful migration requires more than moving records: it requires proving that source and target states reconcile and that critical controls remain intact.

## Objective

Design and execute a repeatable validation framework for large-scale migration / transformation while preserving data consistency and traceability.

## José Carlos's contribution / ownership

- analyzed source / target structures and business definitions
- defined reconciliation and data-quality checks
- compared migrated outputs against expected controls
- investigated discrepancies and outliers
- documented validation logic and findings
- supported cross-platform traceability
- communicated migration risk and data-quality results to technical / business stakeholders
