# ATM and Physical-Network Analytics

## Summary

A portfolio of 2021–2022 analytics for cash demand, ATM maintenance and physical-network decisions. This is the clearest bridge between early banking operations work and later customer/commercial analytics.

## Business problems

- forecast banknote/coin demand across financial subsidiaries
- reduce manual operational work and data-refresh error
- support ATM/branch placement, redistribution or removal decisions
- diagnose remote devices and refine geospatial footprint recommendations
- modernize recurring analytical components

## Contribution

Evidence supports:

- evaluation/adaptation of ML and ETL processes for cash-demand forecasting
- Python-based forecasting for infrastructure demand/capacity
- contribution to predictive-maintenance and device/component analysis
- clustering, market-potential analysis and data visualization for physical-network decisions
- automated ingestion/refresh of recurring ATM status files and dashboards
- support and calibration of footprint tooling, including resolution of a geographic-indexing issue
- delivery of diagnostics and KPI reporting

The evidence varies by subproject: participation is strongest overall; full authorship is not assumed for every underlying model.

## Technologies and methods

- Python, PySpark and SQL
- Teradata and corporate data platforms
- clustering and geospatial analysis
- forecasting / time-series reasoning
- ETL and recurring automation
- Tableau and other BI reporting
- model modernization and validation

## Public outcome

- `ACH-BBVA-ATM-001`: annual savings per redistributed ATM

Other internal portfolio-level figures remain restricted and are not published in this repository.

## Approved public narrative

> Evaluated and adapted forecasting, clustering and ETL workflows for ATM/branch strategy, contributing to a decision-support model with publicly reported annual savings per redistributed ATM.
